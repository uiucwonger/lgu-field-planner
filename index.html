    Field inventory

    Edit any cell; changes save automatically and update the analysis. Status is calculated from comp use, permit and renewal date. Choose a value in the Status column to set it manually, or pick Auto to go back to the calculated value. Capacity used is fixed: your Planning cap if set, otherwise the Baseline cap (largest group seen Oct 19–23). Moving or removing sessions never changes it. Daily windows use 24-hour HH:MM–HH:MM, one per line. Click a column name to sort (again to reverse, a third time to clear). Use the box under it to filter: choose a value, or type text; numbers accept >100, <50, 50-150; (blank) and (not blank) work on every column.

    How the analysis works

    • Status: a field with competitive use is Lapsed if its renewal date is before the as-of date, Expiring within the warning window, and Pending permit if the permit is pending. Lapsed and Lost fields are not usable. A status set manually on the Field inventory tab replaces the calculated one until it is set back to Auto.
    • Capacity: fixed per field. The planning cap if set; otherwise the baseline cap (the largest group of competitive and GK players on the field at once, Oct 19–23). Moving or removing sessions never changes it.
    • Team sessions: the number of distinct days with a non-canceled booking. A session is secure on a usable field and at risk on a lapsed or lost field. Gap = target − secure.
    • Optimize / placements: turf-only programs (ECNL, ECNL RL by default) are placed first, then teams with the biggest gap and roster. Each session goes on a usable field with an allowed surface and enough headroom, on a day the team doesn't already train securely, and preferably not the day before or after another session. A session on a lapsed or lost field can be moved to a usable field on the same day.
    • Data is saved in this browser (localStorage). Export JSON to back up or share.
